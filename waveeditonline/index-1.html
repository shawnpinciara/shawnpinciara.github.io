WAVEEDIT ONLINE

WaveEdit is the free, open-source wavetable editor developed by Synthesis Technology for the E370 Quad Morphing VCO and E352 Cloud Terrarium VCO Eurorack format wavetable oscillators. WaveEdit wavetables are also compatible with the Piston Honda Mark III from Industrial Music Electronics and the Qu-Bit Chord v2. You can download WaveEdit at http://synthtech.com/waveedit for Mac, Windows and Linux.

This site presents a growing library of free wavetable banks shared by WaveEdit users via the WaveEdit Online tab within the software. All wavetables here are under CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication.

Fairlight String Table

All the waves are string waves from the original Fairlight
archives. No editing to the waves has been done in the
Wave Editor. Waves used from the Fairlight string folders
were all the low strings, mid strings, low and mid violins
and numerous others that matched well to make transitions
from each wave smooth and musically pleasing. At least that
was my goal.

29 November 2020 (Christian (kitsu-3))

Soft Mangrove

Samples from the Mannequins Mangrove Oscillator with some artistic freedom. It represents "Barrel" and "Format" on the X and Y axis. I removed silence samples though so it's not an accurate matrix. Also some of the rougher timbers are not represented and it only has a single "Air" setting. Despite all that it's a super organic wavetable that works well with smoothing or glitch and can produce a nice range of pitches and timbres.
